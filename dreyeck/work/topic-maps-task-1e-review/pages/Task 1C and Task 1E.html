What the review of Task 1E found

The semantic checks agree with 6162ed68 and the reviewer's mutants are killed; the review also states what it does not establish: cumulative allocation rises by about 14 to 15 percent (churn, not peak memory), nothing was measured at high Role or Scope arity, and the fast path is not an unconditional speed-up for every graph shape. The review's verdict covers a local commit of those exact bytes; it does not certify deployment, a HyperDoc re-pin, Linux, Stage 2 or a 2 GiB host.
