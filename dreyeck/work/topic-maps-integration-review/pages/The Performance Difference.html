The Performance Difference

The performance table reads the recorded benchmark files when you open it; nothing is measured now. In short:

  • The unchanged b2eecffa did not finish reading the project CTM in 120 s.
  • 6162ed68 reads it in about 29 s (independent, SBCL 2.6.4), 33 s (packaged SBCL 2.4.10) and 34 s (this step's benchmark of the integrated checkout). Below the 90-second limit.
  • On two corpus slices b2eecffa can finish (111 s and 152 s), 6162ed68 takes half a second, with byte-identical output.
  • The sibling lineage 5a4a668 reads it in 7.6 s, because it also indexes the Association search. The 20-second aspiration is therefore not met by this change alone. The row marked EXPERIMENTAL shows an association index on top (about 9 s); it is not Task 1C and not in this integration.

Where the time goes now

The reviewer's profile: the Association pair search is about 73 % of the read; the new topic search about 6 %; before, the topic comparison was 98 %. The review, section 5.

Timings depend on the machine

All numbers are from one Intel Mac. They move with its load: one Project Workspace run on this revision took 161 s on an overloaded machine and 35 s when repeated alone. A check against the 90-second limit belongs on an idle machine, and a Linux or production-sized host has not been measured.
