Deploying Topic Maps with HyperDoc

This reading reviews an uncommitted candidate: a separate Nix package, hyperdoc-catalog-topic-maps, that serves the HyperDoc Catalog with the independent Topic Maps implementation at commit b2eecffa. It was prepared on dreyeck.ch on top of 53ee9993, the CTM review commit. Nothing of it is committed, pushed, published or deployed.

The candidate is its files (43 when it was first presented, more since the guard was corrected and the package was completed), each identified by the bytes the source holds when you look: its SHA-256 now, its Git blob, whether HEAD holds it, and, for the program and test files, whether these are the bytes the tests ran on. The view also checks that Git's changed paths are exactly the declared ones; a source without .git, such as the package's own, says that it cannot. Once the candidate is committed, the same view says committed in and the commit.

The deployment as project work

The goal is the deployment on dreyeck.ch; this candidate is its local packaging. The deployment's work breakdown, dictionary and plan are proposed, not declared. The Proposed Deployment WBS reads them from their proposals and says where the deployment stands. The Six Open Decisions sets out what you decide before anything is declared, and Promoting the Proposals how it would become Work. Nothing in them is an acceptance.

First finding of this review, corrected

While this reading was being built, a defect of the candidate's first Project Workspace guard came to light: it defined two ASDF methods with qualifiers and specializers that ASDF itself already uses, so loading dreyeck/catalog-topic-maps replaced two of ASDF's own methods instead of adding to them. It was corrected on 2026-10-27: the guard is now an explicit check of the Catalog's startup and defines no method on ASDF. The history, with the records of the defect unchanged; which methods ASDF runs now; the claim.

How each conclusion is known

Every claim says which of four it is. Observed: a recorded run saw it, or this reading sees it when shown. Derived: it follows from source or evaluation, and nothing ran it. Inferred: expected, still to be tested. Open: not done or not known. The recorded runs are from 2026-10-26 (the first version of the guard) and 2026-10-27 (the corrected candidate, and Linux); showing them runs nothing.

  • Observed: the archive is the commit, the snapshot verifies without .git, ASDF loads Topic Maps from it, hyperdoc-catalog is unchanged, the package builds and passes its contract on x86_64-linux, this reading runs in the Linux package, the Project Workspace is refused, the two Topic Maps lineages differ, the first guard replaced two ASDF methods, the corrected one adds none, loopback only, scheme-plus is still refuted, production startup passes on darwin, the compilation-cache correction.
  • Derived: Git-only checks are unchanged elsewhere, plain thunk buttons are not gated, HyperDoc's revision in the package is stated, not verified.
  • Open: the archive is not published, nothing pushed or deployed.
