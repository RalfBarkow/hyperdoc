Promoting the Proposals

How the reviewed proposals would become part of the project, in the smallest steps the existing model allows. There are two stages, each a change of its own, each only on your decision, and each with its own Local Commit Review Guide. Neither stage sets a work status you did not declare, and neither records an acceptance.

Stage A: the proposals recorded in the project, still proposals

This is decision 2(b), after the packaging candidate is committed.

  1. Append the addition, unchanged, to dreyeck/pages/project/Implementation Assignments.html.
  2. Move the draft dictionary to dreyeck/pages/project/Topic Maps Deployment WBS Dictionary.html and list it in dreyeck.asd among the HyperBook's drafts (beside the existing one). In the WBS proposal assignment, change the two data-changes paths to the page paths: a path in dreyeck/pages/project/ becomes a page Topic (the mapping). Like the existing draft, it stays outside the CTM's sources.
  3. Regenerate hyperdoc-project.ctm and its .sha256 with the emitter's write-project-ctm, in the 5a4a668 environment, never in this image; then run dreyeck/work/project/tests there. The recorded reading already shows that the emitter reads the page with the addition without refusal, with the gaps it derives for proposals.
  4. Point this reading at the page instead of its own copy: in proposal-sources, the addition becomes the part of the page from its heading "Proposed Work: Deploying Topic Maps with HyperDoc on dreyeck.ch" on, and the dictionary its new path. Remove proposals/'s two files; the cross-check is no longer needed, as the project tests read the page.

The files changed: the page, the CTM and its digest, dreyeck.asd, the moved dictionary, and this reading. Nothing is declared.

Stage B: declaration, package by package

Only on your decision to declare, and only for what you declare.

  1. Declare the Work Item tmd on the Work Breakdown, already a source of the CTM, with data-topic, data-kind and the data-status you give it.
  2. Declare the breakdown and the plan on a carrier Work page of their own, as Authoring the CTM Plan Page carries its WBS. Write its deliverables, work packages, plan steps and preconditions with the same IDs, its statements with data-from, data-relation and data-to, and its completeness and overlap argument. The page needs an entry in dreyeck.asd and in the emitter's +sources+ as a Work source: a change to the emitter's code.
  3. Make the dictionary a declared WBS Dictionary: remove "Draft" and add it to +sources+ as a dictionary. A declared dictionary's entries must name declared packages (D5).
  4. Remove the declared Topics' proposal anchors and the proposed statements from Implementation Assignments; otherwise the emitter reports each one as stale (proposal-already-declared). Keep the assignments: their work-item and delivers references then name declarations.
  5. Regenerate the CTM in the 5a4a668 environment; run the project's tests and the CTM plan page's tests.

Only after Stage B can a record on an Acceptance Records page decide on a package, against the criterion revision it names. Passed tests, completed assignments and executors' reports stay evidence.

What does not change

The IDs, the assignments and their evidence entries, the candidate, its recorded evidence and the collision record. The deployment's next real step is not part of either stage: correcting the guard, which needs your authorization to change tested files (step 5).
