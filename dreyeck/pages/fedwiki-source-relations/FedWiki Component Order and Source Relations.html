6. Loading contract and assessment

The current task is to compile, load, and then use the complete hyperbook/fedwiki system. The serial ASDF order places fedwiki.lisp before story-items.lisp. Consequently, *NEIGHBORHOOD* is declared before story-items.lisp is compiled, while the forward call to PROCESS-TEXT-AND-LINKS initially occurs only inside a function body and its target exists after the complete system has loaded.

Under that load-and-use task, the observed mutual source use is therefore evidence of coupling, but not by itself a demonstrated loading failure. Independent component loading, one-way provider relations, helper separation, or eliminating compiler forward references would be stronger requirements that need their own motivation.

8. Refactoring hypotheses

Observation and proposal remain separate. The current observation records two proposals, both explicitly :NOT-EXECUTED.

Inspect the two refactoring hypotheses

  1. Move FIND-EXAMPLES-FOR-STORY-ITEM-TYPES to a later development/observation component. This would remove the development-helper reverse source relation if the helper is not required during compilation or loading.
  2. Extract the Wiki-text parser as one coherent earlier component. The observed unit consists of *URL-REGEX* , *ANY-EXCEPT-CLOSING-BRACKET-REGEX* , *LINK-REGEX* , and PROCESS-TEXT-AND-LINKS . Moving only the function would leave its parser dependencies behind.

A DECLAIM or forward declaration could document a compiler contract, but it would not remove the observed source dependency.
