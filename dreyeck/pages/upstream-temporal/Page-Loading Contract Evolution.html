Page-Loading Contract Evolution

dreyeck/upstream-intake/temporal

Five upstream commits turned page loading into a protocol. Read one at a time they look like five comparable moves, and the last one — 8a11491 — looks like the one that fell short, since it changes nothing but two lines of a package definition. Read as a run of states it is the opposite: it is the only point at which LOAD-PAGE becomes reachable as a supported export, and everything it needed was already there.

This page shows the same subject in several projections. None of them is the subject. The observed history is, and each projection below is derived from it rather than written out again.

As D2

The Topicmap projection serialized to D2. The shape is ordinary — one line per topic, one per association, nothing but id: "label" and from -> to: "type".

The identifiers are not ordinary, and it is worth knowing why before looking. Each one is the Topic id encoded character by character, six hex digits apiece, so that a node's identity survives any change of label and can be decoded back to the object it stands for. A commit topic's id is git-commit: followed by a full hash, which makes its encoded form several hundred characters long. That is the price of an identity that is shared with every other projection of the same commit rather than local to this diagram, and it is paid in readability. A later slice could offer short identifiers alongside the encoded ones; it would have to keep the SVG identity check in step, which is why it is not done here.

Laid out by TALA

D2 is the language; TALA is one layout choice within it, pinned here to D2 v0.9.0 with the bundled engine. The image is not interactive: no node carries a link or an action, and clicking one does nothing. Its identity is nonetheless preserved and checked — every expected Topic and Association appears as an SVG group keyed by its encoded id — so the rendering can be inspected as evidence even though it is not an interface.

How far apart the states are

The intervals are not in the diagram. A graph layout would have to either ignore them or imply a metric it does not have, and these particular numbers are the reason the dates were recorded at all: the mechanism and the contract that publishes it are five hundred and forty-two days apart, while two of the contract commits are twenty-five seconds apart and are better read as one editing move than as two steps.

The observed dates and the intervals derived from them

What is observed and what is not

  • commit dates and ancestry are observed, re-checked against the object database on every reading;
  • the intervals are derived — a subtraction of two observed instants, neither a new fact nor an interpretation;
  • the capabilities A to F are interpreted, and each capability topic carries the attribution, so following one arrives at its basis rather than at a label.

The capability matrix remains the authority on what was true when. The diagram shows which state established what; it does not decide it.
