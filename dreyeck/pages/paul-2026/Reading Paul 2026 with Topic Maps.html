Reading Paul 2026 with Topic Maps

Taylor H. Paul (2026), A Network Model for the Automated Planning and Scheduling of Pipelined Workflows, University of Maryland, College Park. This is a bounded, source-backed reading. It does not load or run Paul's WORKSWORLD code.

The dissertation defines a resource graph of sites and links (Definition 3.4.4, pp. 66–67, Figure 3.7) and a workflow request graph of alternating data and processing components (Definitions 3.4.5–3.4.6, pp. 68–70). Chapter 4 introduces WORKSWORLD and WORKSWORLD-2 for numeric planning, and WORKSWORLD-2H for SHOP3 HTN planning (pp. 78–98).

WORKSWORLD-2H's main decomposition follows achieve-all-goals → select-goal-interface → plan-source-coverage → plan-source-delivery → commit-rate-flow (Figure 4.2, Table 4.9, pp. 91–95). The delivery method considers reuse, replicate and transform cases (Table 4.11, pp. 95–97). These are claims about Paul's model, not instructions to mutate HyperDoc.

What is merged?

The authored HyperDoc context and the dissertation excerpt are two distinct CTM documents. Both explicitly identify the SHOP3 subject with urn:hyperdoc:subject:shop3. The existing topic-maps.tmdm:import-map-content copies the second Map's content into the first and merges the two SHOP3 Topics by Subject Identifier, preserving native Topic Map semantics, canonical references and navigable Association/Role items. The merge may replace previous Topic handles; use topic-maps.tmdm:canonical-topic to resolve them. These identifiers are local to this reading, not standard PSIs. It is a semantic merge, not a Git-branch merge or a merge of HyperDoc's existing Work Topic objects.

Select the Native Topic Map view and click Read and merge CTM sources. Inspect the returned native TMDM Map in the existing Inspector. Follow Topic → Roles played → Association → Roles → Role player; the native objects, not a second projection graph, hold the relationships.

Or, in the SLY REPL:

(asdf:load-system "dreyeck/work/paul-2026")
(defparameter *paul-map*
  (dreyeck/work/paul-2026:read-merged-map))
(clog-moldable-inspector:clog-inspect :object *paul-map*)

This example is optional and is not part of normal Catalog startup. Reading creates an in-memory Map only. The two CTM files and the Lisp runtime's other objects are unchanged. No WORKSWORLD plan has been executed or performance claim independently reproduced.
