From PageFold to Discourse Graph

The example has two Wiki pages on one site, companion.example. Question holds a pagefold Claims, one paragraph [[Claim A]], and a Mech item NEIGHBORS / EXTRACT / TRAVERSE claim / SOLO; Claim A holds one paragraph.

The run is retained. The tested bundle (19b14c31…) ran once in Node with this fixture and without network access, and its output was recorded. Reading this page re-runs nothing: no Claim Link Survey, no browser and no Wiki is involved. Inspect the run and its stages.

Three different things

The observed link is page content: the story item text [[Claim A]] after a pagefold whose text is Claims. The observed link.

The derived relation is what EXTRACT makes of it. extract_edges_from_story takes the role from the fold label, normalized by canonicalize_fold. The result is one edge, question → claim-a, of type claim. It names pages; it does not record which story item held the link. The EXTRACT stage.

The asserted relation would be the author's claim that Claim A answers Question. Nothing in this run represents or checks it. It is kept as an explicit not-established object.

The stages

  1. resolveEdges finds both pages in the declared site's sitemap and marks them resolved.
  2. traverse follows the role claim from Question and keeps the one edge.
  3. presentGraph builds two WikiPage nodes named site/slug and one relation of type claim.
  4. SOLO posts one batch whose graph is that graph, to /plugins/solo/dialog/#. What Solo then draws is outside this record.
