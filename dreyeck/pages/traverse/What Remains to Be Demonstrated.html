What Remains to Be Demonstrated?

Here Ward's three stages are measured against what the retained pages and the replay show. Anything that is not shown is named as open, not inferred.

Shown

  • The Claim Template exists with the four folds, and other pages reuse its fold items. The claim fold of How I Use My Wiki has the template's item id.
  • The probe exists. One survey was stored, on 2026-01-21.
  • WALK selects by role, and TRAVERSE follows typed edges from a page; both were replayed on the retained pages. A traversal with one resolved relation was handed to SOLO.
  • The deployed sites serve the bundle that was replayed: see What the Deployment Proves and From Git Revision to Nix Package.

Not shown

  • Collaboration across sites. Ward asked for "cooperating federated wiki sites". Every replay ran in one neighborhood, discourse.dreyeck.ch.
    • neighborhood(want) keeps only domains that contain its argument, so NEIGHBORS discourse drops the roster's wow.dreyeck.ch.
    • Zach's Lineup has 49 question links to wiki.planetnineisaspaceship.com. Computed from the recorded edges, every one of those targets stays unresolved.
    So processing across sites is not demonstrated. It would need a concrete case: two cooperating sites in one neighborhood, a typed link from one to the other, and a resolved, traversed relation between them.
  • The pod and its cadence. The retained pages do not show three committed collaborators, a site for each, or weekly reviews.
  • The threshold of stage three. In the snapshot, 6 pages have links under a question fold and 3 under a claim fold, as the role WALK replay selects them. That is short of ten questions and thirty claims.
  • Behaviour in the browser. No observation of these Mech items running on the production Wiki was supplied. The replay shows what the bundle does with the retained bytes, not what a reader saw.
  • Claims about the subject matter. That a page answers a question stays asserted, not verified.

The Experiment/History projection ends both open questions in topics marked not demonstrated and asserted.
