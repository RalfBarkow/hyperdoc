Reading an Actual Discourse Graph

The results on this page come from a replay. The tested bundle (19b14c31…) ran the Mech items of the retained pages in Node. Every page came from the snapshot of 2026-10-21, and the network was disabled. Reading this page runs nothing. The recorded replay.

The replay is not an observation of the production Wiki in a browser; no such observation was supplied. It also assumes a neighborhood that contains discourse.dreyeck.ch. NEIGHBORS discourse would drop any other site whose domain lacks "discourse".

What EXTRACT finds

In 110 pages EXTRACT finds 86 typed edges: 60 question, 16 support, 5 claim and 5 oppose.

  • 19 targets resolve.
  • 18 targets name pages of discourse.dreyeck.ch that its sitemap does not list.
  • 49 targets name another site, so in this neighborhood they cannot resolve.

Why TRAVERSE on Discourse Graphs finds nothing

Discourse Graphs holds a TRAVERSE program. The page has only two pagefolds, . and ~, and neither names a role. Its links, such as [[Claim Template]], come before any fold.

So no edge starts at the page, and TRAVERSE reports that it "found no question/claim/support/oppose relations from discourse.dreyeck.ch+discourse-graphs". SOLO then has no aspect to hand over. The replayed case.

The page proposes the graph; it is not a node of it.

A path that succeeds

Can a Wiki Page Represent a Claim? was written for this on 2026-10-20 (retained). Step by step:

  1. The pagefold Claims is followed by the story item [[A Wiki Page Can Represent a Claim]].
  2. EXTRACT derives the edge with the role claim.
  3. Its target is listed in the sitemap, so resolveEdges marks it resolved.
  4. TRAVERSE claim reports 1 relations, 2 nodes, 0 unresolved, and SOLO receives one batch.

Each step with its value and its JavaScript definition: the fold, the edge, the TRAVERSE result, the SOLO batch. The whole path.

Derived is not asserted

How I Use My Wiki also has a claim fold. One of its items links to Claim Link Survey, so TRAVERSE claim yields the relation How I Use My Wiki —claim→ Claim Link Survey.

The role comes from where the link stands, not from what the target says. That A Wiki Page Can Represent a Claim answers its question is a claim that nothing here checks.
