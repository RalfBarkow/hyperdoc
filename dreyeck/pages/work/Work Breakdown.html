Why: use HyperDoc itself to keep questions, work, explanations, executable or observed evidence, dependencies and next decisions connected and inspectable, while distinguishing what is implemented, what is deployed, what is only intended and what remains unresolved. Relation contracts

A relation written work:relation/… refers to the one Topic below with that ID. The Topic ID is the contract's identity; the link text is its label; the linked page states what the relation asserts. Other relations are still plain words.

  • informs — draft.
  • addresses — the Work item includes investigation, implementation or change of the named Constraint within its intended scope. Source role: work item; target role: constraint. This does not assert that the Constraint is satisfied, deployment is complete, change authority is granted, or evidence is attached to the Work item.
