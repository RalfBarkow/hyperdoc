Relation Contract: informs

dreyeck/work/reading

Status: draft. This page states the meaning of the Work relation whose identity is the Topic ID work:relation/informs. Its label is informs. The title above names this page, not the relation: a new page title or a new label leaves the relation, and every Association that refers to it, unchanged.

What "A informs B" asserts

Results or questions from A provide input relevant to B. Each Association that uses this contract remains its own statement between two Work Topics.

  • Interaction → Operations and change: Interaction records a selected Operation separately from carrying it out; what that Operation may change is a question for Operations and change.
  • Operations and change → Connect / Associations: the Operations and Change page asks which authority each kind of change affects, and names Connect among the work that depends on that distinction.

What it does not assert

Authorization. Work Breakdown says of its arrows that they "do not authorize execution". An informs relation grants no change authority to A, to B or to anyone acting on them.

Open contract questions

The current Work material does not settle these. Until it does, informs asserts none of them.

Execution order
Must work on A come before work on B? No Work page states an order.
Ownership
Does informs say who owns A or B? No Work page connects the relation with ownership.
Strict prerequisite
Work Breakdown records Operations and change → State and persistence separately as requires. The Operations and Change page also says Connect depends on its distinction, while Work Breakdown records that edge as informs. Whether informs ever carries a prerequisite is open.
Causal determination
Does a result of A determine B? No Work page claims so.

Uses

Associations using this contract, read from Work Breakdown. Each is a separate Association with its own ID.

Whether changing an Association's relation replaces it with another Association or changes one stable Association is not decided here. Today an Association's ID spells its relation, so a retyped Association gets a new ID.
