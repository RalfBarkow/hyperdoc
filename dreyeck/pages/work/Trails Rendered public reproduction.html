dreyeck/work/trails-rendered-reading

Ward Cunningham reported incremental work on a project he now calls "layers of interpretation", with a snippet that finds a Graphviz node by its <title> and asks for its getBBox(). This reading records where that work can be traced in public, and a local Federated Wiki that reproduces his public Trails Rendered page up to the point where the public sources end.

The snippet was not found in any public source. Ward states that the change was made in an experimental Solo popup deployed only to his own localhost. The reproduction therefore stops before that change, and says so.
