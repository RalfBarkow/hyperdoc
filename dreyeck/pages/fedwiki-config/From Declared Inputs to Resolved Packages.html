Different mechanisms choose the other clients

Dreyeck uses hash-pinned fetchers in its recipe for Wiki, client and server. Its root flake lock does not itself pin those component fetchers. It preserves an npm-installed browser bundle while replacing client source, so the source revision and served bundle digest are separate evidence objects.

P41 reconstructs package inputs from upstream and personal archives plus recorded patches, then builds its client. Its root flake lock selects nixpkgs and the shared Mech library. Client inputs nested inside that library's lock do not select the P41 client. Read the P41 source-inputs, patches and package provenance to establish that selection.
