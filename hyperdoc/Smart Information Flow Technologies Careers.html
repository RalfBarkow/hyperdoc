Source claim

The pasted page presents SIFT as a growing research and development company based in Minneapolis, Minnesota, with staff in Boston, San Francisco, Dallas, San Diego, and Washington DC. The page frames SIFT interviews as exposure to many interesting research projects and invites candidates from experienced researchers through interns.

Research and development frame

The source describes SIFT as working across Natural Language Processing, Automated Planning, Cybersecurity, Supervisory Control, Healthcare, and Human-Automation Interaction. It emphasizes a mixed research/development workflow: requirements elicitation, design, implementation, testing and evaluation, research, proposal writing, and concept development.

Skill matrix extracted from the source

  • Development/engineering: Python, Lisp, HTML5, CSS, NodeJS, constraint programming, and functional programming such as SML, OCaml, or Haskell.
  • Key research: AI planning methods, plan recognition, NLP, computational linguistics, machine learning, and constraint satisfaction/optimization.
  • Valued adjacent research: formal methods, control theory, operations-research optimization, human/computer interaction, UI development, and simulation.
  • Program-development growth path: proposal preparation and program management are pluses, with full-time employees able to grow into securing or leading R&D programs.

Employment boundary

The pasted source states that, due to contract work, SIFT positions are limited to United States citizens or permanent residents. The same source includes an equal-opportunity statement covering age, race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, pregnancy, protected veteran status, and disability.
