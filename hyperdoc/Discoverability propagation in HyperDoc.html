Discoverability propagation in HyperDoc

hyperdoc

Discoverability propagation is the second documentary move in the skill pattern. It adds one-click orientation from adjacent surfaces to an already-landed conceptual center.

The preferred form is a docs-only propagation slice: compact cross-links, short orientation cues, and no renewed topic growth unless that need is explicit and unavoidable.

Inspectable objects

Conservative rule

The second slice should usually not reopen theory, redesign runtime mechanics, or grow topics again. Its job is narrower: make the conceptual center easier to find from the pages where readers already arrive.

The main failure mode is architectural drift, where a discoverability slice quietly turns back into a new conceptual-center slice. The safest countermeasure is to keep the propagation slice page-local and compact.
