Autonomous Weapons Resilience Playbook

This playbook focuses on civilian harm reduction in environments where autonomous weapon systems and related infrastructure disruption are persistent risks. It is not a combat guide.

Inspectable objects

  • Autonomous-weapons civilian resilience
  • Autonomous-threat risk model
  • Protective infrastructure hardening
  • Civilian alerting fallback channels
  • Disinformation verification loop
  • Continuity of care under disruption
  • Autonomous-weapons governance accountability

Playbook layers

  1. Preparedness: map risks, dependencies, shelters, and critical contacts.
  2. Detection and warning: maintain trusted, redundant alert channels.
  3. Protection: prioritize non-combatant sheltering and medical continuity.
  4. Continuity: keep minimum services functioning under degraded conditions.
  5. Recovery: restore institutions, records, and accountability trails.
