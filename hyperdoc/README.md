# Zero-knowledge-proofs HyperDoc starter slice

This starter bundle contains a first repo-native HyperDoc slice derived from
page 1 of the `History of Zero Knowledge Proofs` FedWiki text.

## Included pages

- `Zero Knowledge Proofs.html`
- `Trust Without Disclosure.html`
- `Social Authentication Before Cryptography.html`
- `Confession, Privacy, and Trusted Speech.html`
- `Martin Guerre and the Problem of Identity.html`

## Included Lisp handoff

- `zkp-topics-snippet.lisp`

## Editorial intent

This slice starts from the social and historical prehistory emphasized in page 1
of the source text. It deliberately does **not** yet try to cover the full
mathematical history from Shannon to RSA or formal interactive proof systems.

## Existing HyperDoc cluster used as neighbors

The pages are written to connect into the already-existing cluster around:

- Digital Identity as Abstraction
- Over-Identification and Identity Creep
- Digital Identity and Social Engineering
- Trust Erosion from Identity Fraud
- The False Security of Biometrics
- Design Constraints for Public Digital Identity
- Swiss E-ID Through the Lens of Digital Identity Risk
