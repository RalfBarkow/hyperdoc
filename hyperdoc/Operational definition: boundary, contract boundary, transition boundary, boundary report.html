Operational definition: boundary, contract boundary, transition boundary, boundary report

hyperdoc

A boundary is a bounded transition or contract edge where one mode of access, representation, authority, or execution gives way to another.

A HyperDoc boundary is defined not only by what it separates, but by:

  • the two sides it relates
  • the condition for crossing it
  • the operations allowed before and after it
  • the evidence emitted at the crossing
  • the failure classifications that can block it
  • the adjacent surfaces or stages that it connects

A boundary may be static or dynamic:

  • contract boundary: describes what counts as valid access or representation
  • transition boundary: describes a stage crossing during a concrete run

In HyperDoc, a boundary must exist at two levels:

  1. a reusable definition object
  2. a concrete boundary instance

A boundary report is related but narrower: it summarizes unresolved or blocked outcomes at or around one authoritative boundary without replacing the boundary object itself.
