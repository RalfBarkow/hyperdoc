Fully Democratic Co-determination Prose

hyperdoc

Fully Democratic Co-determination Prose names a writing form in which contributions can enter at any point in the text and can alter not only wording but also the developing theory itself. The form matters less as a literary genre than as a communication arrangement: authorship is decentralized, revision is expected, and the text remains open to further internal differentiation.

Why this belongs in HyperDoc

HyperDoc already treats documentation as a live communication surface rather than a finished book alone. This topic adds a sharper name for a stronger claim: not merely collaborative editing, but a prose form whose legitimacy comes from equal access to revision and from the continued negotiability of the theory carried by the text.

That makes the topic relevant to HyperDoc whenever a page is not only reporting a settled result, but is also serving as a shared site where a concept is reformulated, tightened, and redistributed across linked pages and topics.

Precision and readability

The key tension is not simply between good and bad writing. A fully democratic co-determination prose can privilege theoretical precision over immediate readability, because each intervention may sharpen distinctions, qualify earlier claims, or reopen a formulation that had looked stable.

In that sense, readability and precision can themselves become observable selections inside the writing process rather than external standards imposed once and for all.

Second-order observation

The topic becomes especially useful when the writing process observes its own selectivity. One can ask not only what a passage says, but also why one formulation, distinction, or sequencing choice was preferred over another. The prose then carries both first-order claims and second-order observations about how those claims were stabilized.

Relation to Parallel Poetry

Parallel Poetry names a neighboring form in which multiple lines of expression may remain adjacent rather than being collapsed immediately into one authoritative wording. Fully democratic co-determination prose is the broader collaborative-theoretical problem; parallel poetry is one of the expressive arrangements that can preserve divergence long enough for further selection.
