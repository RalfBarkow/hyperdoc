The Reel as Accessible Carousel

hyperdoc

Page genre: reference/contract. HyperDoc inspector panes use Every Layout's Reel as the layout primitive and BBC GEL's carousel guidance as the accessibility contract. This is not an autoplay carousel; it is a user-controlled horizontal inspector surface.

Claim

HyperDoc inspector panes should be horizontally navigable as a browser-native Reel with accessible carousel controls, not as an opaque custom carousel widget.

  • Every Layout's Reel gives the horizontal overflow layout primitive.
  • BBC GEL gives the user-controlled carousel accessibility contract.
  • Robert Best's scroll-snap lineup and Paul Rodwell's page-local component reorder are prior FedWiki CSS-native layout experiments.

Inspector Contract

  • Panes remain in document order.
  • Horizontal overflow remains native for touch, trackpad, wheel, shift-wheel, scrollbar, and keyboard focus.
  • Previous and next buttons enhance the native scroll container; they are real button type="button" controls.
  • Buttons are hidden until JavaScript initializes, then scroll by half the scrollable viewport width.
  • Boundary buttons disable at the left and right scroll limits.
  • Less-than-half-visible panes become inert when browser support allows it; fallback focus management keeps obscured panes from trapping keyboard focus.
  • Reduced-motion preference is respected.
  • Autoplay must never be introduced.

Implementation Surface

  • hyperbook-server/inspector-performance.lisp creates section.hyperdoc-reel, hidden controls, and the scrollable .inspector.hyperdoc-reel__scrollable adapter.
  • assets/hyperdoc/css/hyperdoc-reel.css provides the namespaced Reel layout, snap affordance, reduced-motion rule, and high-contrast boundaries.
  • assets/hyperdoc/js/hyperdoc-reel.js initializes every .hyperdoc-reel, reveals controls, debounces scroll updates, tracks boundary buttons, and applies inertness.

Goldberg Questions

What is this component?
A user-controlled horizontal inspector pane Reel.
What problem does it solve?
It gives multiple inspector panes a perceivable, accessible horizontal navigation model.
What are its parts?
Group, buttons, scrollable viewport, pane list, pane items, inertness observer.
What happens without JavaScript?
Native horizontal scrolling remains usable.
What happens with JavaScript?
Buttons, inertness, and boundary disabling are added.
What must never happen?
Autoplay, focus traps in obscured panes, or a custom widget that suppresses native scrolling.

Boundary

This contract covers the inspector pane row. It does not replace Dock identity, pane body route traversal, Annotation, or Touch-Fahrplan route-laying. The Reel controls move through existing inspector panes; command dispatch and pane content semantics remain owned by the inspector and HyperDoc runtime.
