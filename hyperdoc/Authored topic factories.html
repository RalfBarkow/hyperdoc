Authored topic factories

hyperdoc

Authored topic factories are the preserved topic-constructor functions in hyperdoc/topics.lisp that keep HyperDoc's topic-page surface rebuildable. They are the authoring layer that remains available even when selected similarly named wrapper entry points are redirected to live DMX-backed runtime proxies.

What they are

An authored topic factory is a durable function such as (defun topic-factory-topic () ...) that returns a topic object with a stable key, canonical title, summary, and editorial references. Together, these functions form the authored reconstruction layer for HyperBook topics.

Why they are preserved

HyperDoc still needs a stable authored source for rebuilding topic pages by canonical title. That need remains even when some runtime-facing entry points are later redirected to DMX-backed proxies for inspector use.

How they differ from runtime wrappers

A runtime wrapper may return a live DMX-backed object for inspection. An authored topic factory remains the durable authoring function that preserves the topic-page surface. The two layers can share conceptual names without being the same runtime object.

Where they matter

They matter whenever HyperDoc needs to keep canonical topic titles stable and rebuild the Topics HyperBook surface from authored concept definitions rather than from transient runtime state.
