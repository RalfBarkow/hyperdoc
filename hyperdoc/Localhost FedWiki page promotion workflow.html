Localhost FedWiki page promotion workflow

hyperdoc

Localhost FedWiki page promotion workflow is the reusable operational path for reading a real localhost FedWiki page, normalizing its story items and fragments, promoting topic-shaped chunks, composing a durable local HyperDoc page, and preparing a separate dry-run-first DMX topic-factory snippet twin.

Workflow boundary

The plan object is the inspectable boundary. It surfaces the localhost source page id, slug, repo-relative path, normalized story items, normalized fragments, promoted topic chunks, provenance classifications, local page target, topic-factory snippet metadata, and the separate DMX dry-run plan summary.

Execution boundaries

  1. inspect/plan: open a promotion plan object's Overview, Source page, Story items, Fragments, and Promoted topics views.
  2. compose/write local HyperDoc artifacts: use (write-localhost-fedwiki-page-promotion-plan-artifacts plan) to regenerate the durable local page and snippet asset from the plan's configured writer.
  3. dry-run DMX snippet write: inspect the plan object's DMX dry-run view or call (localhost-fedwiki-page-promotion-plan-dmx-dry-run-summary plan) / (localhost-fedwiki-page-promotion-plan-dmx-dry-run-evidence plan).
  4. optional live DMX write: remain on the separate explicit writer path through (execute-topic-factory-snippet-dmx-write ... :dry-run nil) with an explicit workspace topicmap id. The authored HyperDoc page stays local and durable either way.

Current real-page instances

The current two real localhost FedWiki promotions intentionally exercise different provenance modes while sharing the same generic mechanism.

  • The Life Cycle of Collective Knowledge: one paragraph story item is split into fragment-derived reusable topics, so the promoted chunks expose story-item-fragment provenance.
  • Reproducible DevEnv as Knowledge Artifact: the two promoted subtopics stay whole-item-derived while the umbrella topic exposes multi-item-derived provenance across both paragraph items.
