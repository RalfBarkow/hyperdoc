Operational definition: surface, diagnostic surface, mutation surface, failure surface

hyperdoc

A surface is a bounded operational interface through which a user or robot can inspect, diagnose, act on, or publish some aspect of a HyperDoc-managed system.

A surface is defined not only by what it shows, but by:

  • its scope
  • its permitted operations
  • its evidence/output forms
  • its boundary conditions
  • its correspondence to adjacent surfaces

A HyperDoc surface may be authored/durable, inspectable/runtime, diagnostic/read-only, mutation/action-bearing, or publication-oriented, but these are explicit classifications rather than interchangeable synonyms.

In HyperDoc, a surface must exist at two levels:

  1. a reusable definition object
  2. a concrete surface instance
