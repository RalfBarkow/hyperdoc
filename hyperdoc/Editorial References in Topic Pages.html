Editorial References in Topic Pages

hyperdoc

This note records the current decision about optional editorial :references on HyperDoc topic objects.

Inspectable objects

  • Editorial references in topic pages
  • Topic editorial references
  • Topic backlinks model

Why this note exists

After the Topics HyperBook refactor, internal topic-to-page associations are no longer primarily curated through :references. This note clarifies what editorial references still mean and whether they remain part of the reader-facing topic page contract.

Backlinks and editorial references are different

  • Derived relation: backlinks derived from authored incoming links that point to a topic page.
  • Curated context: editorial references stored on the topic object.

Backlinks answer “which pages point here?” Editorial references answer “which extra references should stay attached to this topic as curated context?”

Decision

Editorial :references remain optional reader-visible curated context, but they are no longer the primary mechanism for internal topic-to-page association.

Current UI status

Topic pages in the current Topics HyperBook render an Editorial references section. That means editorial references are currently part of the reader-facing topic page contract, not hidden internal metadata.

What editorial references are for

  • external URLs that should stay attached to the topic
  • supplementary curated HyperDoc pages that add context but are not merely discovered incoming links
  • historical or editorial wayfinding that should remain visible even if backlink structure changes

What editorial references are not for

  • they are not the authoritative source of internal topic-to-page association
  • they are not a replacement for authored page-to-topic links
  • they are not a separate inference layer or topic graph

Maintenance guidance

  • Add editorial references only when curated context adds value beyond backlinks.
  • Do not duplicate every backlink manually in :references.
  • Prefer backlinks for internal association, editorial references for optional curation.
  • If a topic page’s editorial references stop adding reader value, they can be reduced without changing the backlink model.
