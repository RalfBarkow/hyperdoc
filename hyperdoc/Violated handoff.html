Violated handoff

hyperdoc

A violated handoff is a collaboration failure mode: a sender declares a transfer complete, but the receiver cannot continue without re-deriving hidden context.

Inspectable objects

  • Violated handoff
  • Express both sides of a handoff without manually reversing perspective
  • Surface Answer
  • Artifact Answer
  • Reconstruction protocol

Operational definition

  • Claimed completion without required artifacts.
  • Missing links between surface claim and durable objects.
  • No replayable verification path for the receiver.

Minimum repair

  • State what was done (process trace).
  • Point to exact artifact deltas (files/pages/topics/twins).
  • Provide replay checks that prove current state.
