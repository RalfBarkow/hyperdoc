Boundary report

hyperdoc

A Boundary report is a reporting object that summarizes what an execution or workflow could not resolve, represent, or complete cleanly at its authoritative seam. It is related to a boundary, but it is not the same thing as a boundary definition or boundary instance.

Inspectable objects

  • (make-dmx-workspace-journal-preflight-blocked-boundary-instance) Evidence
  • (make-dmx-workspace-journal-preflight-blocked-boundary-instance) Failure analysis

How it differs from a boundary

  • Boundary definition: states the sides, crossing condition, allowed operations, evidence, and failure classes.
  • Boundary instance: records one concrete crossing or block.
  • Boundary report: summarizes unresolved, blocked, or lossy outcomes at or around those boundaries.

That is why a report belongs beside runtime dispatch seams, execution evidence objects, and runtime provenance. It is part of the evidence and explanation layer, not the crossing contract itself.
