Why keep this as a page

The current Drew boundary is concept-and-software oriented. The book title is therefore best kept as a durable source page that points into reusable concepts, rather than becoming a first-class topic in its own right.

Source-backed claims in this slice

  • The book argues for a computational explanation of mind and treats cognitive science as the study of computational models of mental work.
  • It treats some representations as behavior-changing structures rather than passive descriptions, which is why its examples move quickly from stored models to what a system does next.
  • It introduces informational meaning as an evidence-like relation and treats intentionality and semantics as questions about symbol structures with causal and behavioral consequences.
  • It explains introspective phenomena through self-models and explicitly allows that a useful self-model need not be perfectly true.
