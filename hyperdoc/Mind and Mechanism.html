Mind and Mechanism

hyperdoc

Drew V. McDermott's Mind and Mechanism belongs in HyperDoc as a book-level source page, not as a cue to import one topic per chapter or one topic per index entry. Its reusable value for this repository lies in a small set of compatibility seams around computational explanation, symbols and semantics, intentionality, informational meaning, and self-modeling.

Those seams matter here because the existing Drew slice already preserves a concrete software and planning cluster: YTools, Lexiparse, Nisp, Nity, Opt, Optop, PDDL, ontology translation, estimated-regression planning, autonomous process, and running-image coherence. The book gives that cluster a broader computational backdrop without changing the import boundary.
