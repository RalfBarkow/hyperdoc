Diagnostic surface

hyperdoc

A Diagnostic surface is a read-only surface whose job is to inspect, compare, classify, or explain evidence without widening into live mutation.

DMX worked example

The current worked example is Diagnosing DMX workspace repair triage. It inspects topicmap 919822, preserves workspace assignment as distinct from topicmap placement, and keeps mutation on the adjacent mutation surface instead of collapsing both roles into one tab.

Boundary rules

  • Diagnosis remains read-only.
  • Evidence forms must stay visible on the surface itself.
  • Adjacent mutation or failure surfaces must stay explicit rather than being implied in prose only.
