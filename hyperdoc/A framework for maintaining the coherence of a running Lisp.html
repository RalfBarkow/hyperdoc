Why the title matters here

HyperDoc repeatedly runs into the question of how a live Lisp image can evolve without losing reconstructability, inspectability, and trust in what is actually loaded. A paper explicitly framed around maintaining the coherence of a running Lisp belongs close to those concerns.

Conservative import stance

  • The site gives the title, year, and venue.
  • It is enough to create a durable page/topic anchor.
  • It is not enough to claim the paper's detailed argument until the paper itself is imported.

So this page should be read as a bridge page: it records relevance, points to existing HyperDoc concerns, and leaves room for a later paper-based expansion.
