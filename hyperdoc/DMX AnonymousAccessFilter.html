DMX AnonymousAccessFilter

hyperdoc

DMX AnonymousAccessFilter is the DMX-core-native fallback filter that decides whether a request prefix may be served anonymously for reads or writes. It is important for learning because it explains why some DMX reads still work without credentials, but it must not be confused with the guarded mutation contract used by the repair console or workspace annotations.

Platform contract

systems.dmx.accesscontrol.AnonymousAccessFilter keeps separate read-prefix and write-prefix settings. Its method isAnonymousAccessAllowed(HttpServletRequest request) checks the request method and then matches the request URI against the configured prefix lists.

This means anonymous access is a fallback outcome after DMX has either not seen usable credentials or not required them for a configured prefix. It is not one of HyperDoc's three primary operator input modes.

Boundary clarification

  • Anonymous fallback can explain why some DMX reads work without login.
  • It does not mean guarded repair or annotation continuation can rely on anonymous mutation.
  • It does not replace object-level ACL, workspace assignment, or topic update permission.
