Procedure

  1. Open the check object's Summary view and read the ancestry and patch-equivalence flags separately.
  2. Open Proof to see the exact read-only commands and key outputs.
  3. Open Graph/History to see what remains unique to the source branch after cherry-equivalent replay.
  4. Open Range-diff to identify the explicit replayed commit hash on the target branch.
  5. Use the merge-intent interpretation sentence as the final readable answer.
