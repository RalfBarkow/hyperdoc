Merge Path Decisions for upstream main into hauptsache

hyperdoc

This page adds the next narrow layer on top of the merge forecast: per-path decisions that can later drive the actual merge, while still avoiding any merge execution in this slice.

The durable fact is a typed git-path-decision object. Overlapping paths get one of four classifications:

  • adopt-upstream
  • keep-hauptsache
  • extract-to-dreyeck
  • manual-merge-needed

Hauptsache-only paths get a second classification pass:

  • clearly-dreyeck-candidate
  • likely-keep-in-hyperdoc-core
  • unknown-needs-review

Views

  • 👀path-decisions on a decision surface lists path, classification, rationale, and linked note.
  • 👀summary on a single git-path-decision shows its current merge rationale and any attached note object.
  • 👀overlapping-path-decisions, 👀dreyeck-candidate-paths, and 👀unresolved-manual-paths on the forecast object expose the same classifications in forecast context.
  • 👀dreyeck-extraction-plan and 👀manual-conflicts turn those classifications into the next two durable merge-preparation artifacts.

Why classify before merging

The goal is not to guess the final merged tree from prose. The goal is to make the next merge-driving decisions inspectable ahead of time, especially where the dreyeck extraction policy is already a plausible answer and where manual union work remains unavoidable.

The next narrow slice after this page is to turn the filtered dreyeck candidates into an extraction plan and to turn the remaining package and ASDF conflicts into a manual-conflict dossier. Those two objects stay downstream preparation artifacts; they still do not execute the merge.
