Mutation surface

hyperdoc

A Mutation surface is an action-bearing surface that performs bounded mutation while keeping scope, authority requirements, visible evidence, and adjacent diagnostic surfaces explicit.

DMX worked example

The current worked example is Using authenticated workspace assignment repair console. It remains a narrow explicit-auth mutation surface rather than a generic DMX mutation shell, and it preserves the adjacent read-only diagnostic and failure surfaces around the same guarded workflow.

Boundary rules

  • Mutation must stay bounded; the surface does not widen into unrestricted backend access.
  • Authority requirements must be explicit.
  • Dry-run and live mutation evidence must remain visible on the surface itself.
