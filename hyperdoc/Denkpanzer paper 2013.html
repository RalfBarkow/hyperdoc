What the paper studies

The paper circles around the transfer of an Anglo-Saxon think-tank model into Swiss conditions and the reaction to that transfer across media and intellectual conflict. Its empirical material is not a single organization but a field of communication events between 1998 and 2012.

Durable concepts extracted from the paper

  • Swiss think-tank model transfer: how an imported organizational pattern becomes visible through adaptation and resistance in a local setting.
  • Power-of-definition conflict: conflict over who gets to define the problem, the acceptable solution, and the meaning of hegemony.
  • Communication intensification as conflict early warning: dense bursts of public communication can act as signals for deeper social conflict.
  • Think-tank ideological temporization: the paper reads ideology as something staged and timed through media attention, not only as a static label.

Boundary

This page does not claim that every think tank works the same way or that the paper's framing settles all ideological or sociological disputes. The narrower HyperDoc use is to preserve reusable distinctions that the work makes available for later inquiry.
