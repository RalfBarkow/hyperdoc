What dreyeck is here

In this repo, dreyeck is the deployed HyperDoc host. The onboarding problem is not just “how to rebuild NixOS,” but how to preserve HyperDoc-specific parity across deployment, restart, and verification.

What can be rehearsed locally

Local verification, release smoke, and runner-based rehearsal should happen before live activation. A newcomer should learn the controller-local path first.

What touches the remote host

Backup, generation recording, restart, and final activation touch the remote host. Public URL probes then confirm what the outside world sees.

What counts as success

  • The service is up.
  • The intended public URLs respond.
  • Scoped example parity is present without rescue loads.

What to do if parity fails

Move from public verification to runtime probing. If the service is under-loaded, fix startup/load scope; if recovery is needed immediately, roll back.
