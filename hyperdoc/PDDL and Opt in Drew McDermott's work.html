PDDL sequence on the site

  • PDDL 1.7: the original competition language.
  • PDDL2.1: adds time and objective functions, while the site notes that some original features were removed for "retuning."
  • PDDL2.2: adds derived predicates and timed initial literals.
  • PDDL3.0: adds constraints and preferences in a restricted temporal logic.

Opt as successor line

The site positions Opt as a successor to PDDL, including durative actions, autonomous processes, a revised hierarchical-planning notation, and a more robust type system. Optop then appears as an estimated-regression planner on top of that language substrate.

Why this is relevant to HyperDoc

This material is useful less as competition history than as an example of how a language family evolves when representational limits become operational limits. It also supplies a bridge between language design, planner behavior, and the software stack described elsewhere on the site.

That bridge is also the most direct compatibility seam to Mind and Mechanism. The book's discussion of symbols, semantics, intentionality, and informational meaning matters here because these languages are important only when their representations change planner behavior rather than remaining notation trivia.
