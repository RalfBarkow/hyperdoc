Girard Linear Logic and Interaction Nets

hyperdoc

This page assimilates Jean-Yves Girard's 1987 paper Linear Logic as a source station for the Interaction nets neighborhood. The paper should not be flattened into an Interaction nets page: its direct objects are linear logic, phase semantics, coherent spaces, proof-nets, trips, cut elimination, and the logical treatment of parallel communication. Interaction nets belong downstream in the same neighborhood through proof-net normalization and local interaction.
