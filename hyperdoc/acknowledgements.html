Acknowledgements

HyperDoc has been influenced by discussions with many people over many years In particular:

  • The Future of Coding community
  • The Malleable Systems Collective
  • The GT Notebook

It has also been influenced by projects that share some of its goals, values, and technical challenges:

  • Glamorous Toolkit is the reference platform for Moldable Development, which has much in common with HyperDoc's goal of conviviality. It also shares HyperDoc's focus on explainable software systems.
  • Freewheeling Apps share HyperDoc's minimalist approach to implementing conviviality.
  • Federated Wiki and Webstrates share HyperDoc's choice of the Web as a platform for publication and sharing, and add an aspect that remains to be developed for HyperDoc: collaboration.
