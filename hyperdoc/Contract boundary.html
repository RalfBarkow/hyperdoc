Contract boundary

hyperdoc

A Contract boundary defines what counts as valid access, representation, or mutation across a stable edge. It is not only a stage in one run; it is the reusable rule that says what may be treated as authoritative on either side.

What a contract boundary must say

  • what lies on the left side
  • what lies on the right side
  • what condition must be satisfied before the crossing counts as valid
  • what operations are permitted and blocked on either side
  • what evidence proves the contract was respected or violated

Worked DMX example

The page DMX note read/write boundary is a concrete contract-boundary example. It keeps three things distinct:

  • the canonical machine-readable parent-note read path
  • the stable parent-note mutation target
  • the post-write verification reread that proves the write result

That makes it broader than a single tool call and narrower than a whole DMX workflow. It is a reusable contract edge.
