Why attach dreyeck notes to the relation

The notes are not generic commentary. They are attached to the concrete merge-intent relation so that merge preparation can point to likely extraction seams in the same object graph that already stores the review anchors, conflict policy, and success criteria.

Blocker interpretation

In this slice, a blocker does not mean "merge impossible." It means "inspect this preparation chain before running any merge command." At the anchored tips that now means resolving the current manual merge-driving frontier of six typed raw conflicts while keeping the 44-path dreyeck plan separate from the shared-core renderer, FedWiki, and Wikipedia conflicts. The current gate is no longer blocked by missing proposal or recipe objects; it is blocked by the quality and later execution of those six shared-core merge recipes. The goal remains preparation and explicit review, not execution.

That interpretation remains true as history, but it is no longer the live branch status after the 2026-03-22 execution slice. The merge-preparation chain remains here as durable provenance for the manual steps that were required.
