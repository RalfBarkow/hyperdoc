Conceptual center in HyperDoc

hyperdoc

A conceptual center is the first documentary move in a good skillization pattern. It lands the imported concept in HyperDoc's own language, fixes the local editorial center, and makes the intended boundary explicit before discoverability work begins.

This first slice may add the minimum new pages and topics required to make the center durable. It should not scatter the idea across adjacent surfaces before the center itself is clear.

Distinct move

The conceptual-center slice is where HyperDoc decides what the local concept is, what vocabulary should remain durable, and what belongs on stable pages rather than only in conversational handover. That makes it different from a later discoverability slice, which should help readers find the center but should not redefine it.
