Kioskbeerli ASDF extraction boundary

hyperdoc

Kioskbeerli is a downstream application/system. HyperDoc may document it and Kioskbeerli may depend on HyperDoc APIs for pages, topics, SCXML, and inspector views, but HyperDoc must not depend on Kioskbeerli.

Boundary

  • Allowed direction: Kioskbeerli depends on HyperDoc APIs.
  • Forbidden direction: HyperDoc depends on :kioskbeerli, :dreyeck/kioskbeerli, or compatibility wrappers in the HYPERDOC package.
  • Documentation-only references: HyperDoc pages may mention Kioskbeerli as a case study when the page does not require loading Kioskbeerli during HyperDoc core load.
  • Load-bearing references: ASDF dependencies, test components, package-prefixed runtime calls, and wrapper functions belong outside HyperDoc core.

Current HyperDoc-side split

HyperDoc no longer loads hyperdoc/kioskbeerli-compat.lisp from the :hyperdoc system. The aggregate :hyperdoc/tests system no longer depends on :kioskbeerli and no longer loads the Kioskbeerli dashboard or DMX associative mirror smoke files.

External target

/Users/rgb/workspace/hauptsache/kioskbeerli

The target already contains the Kioskbeerli Nix flake, NixOS module, README, and operational scripts. The Lisp/ASDF extraction should move the Kioskbeerli ASDF definition and Lisp tree there in a separate migration slice.

Migration plan

  1. Move /Users/rgb/workspace/hyperdoc/kioskbeerli.asd to /Users/rgb/workspace/hauptsache/kioskbeerli/kioskbeerli.asd.
  2. Move /Users/rgb/workspace/hyperdoc/kioskbeerli/ to /Users/rgb/workspace/hauptsache/kioskbeerli/kioskbeerli/.
  3. Keep Kioskbeerli ASDF dependencies pointed at public HyperDoc systems, not private HyperDoc compatibility wrappers.
  4. Move Kioskbeerli-specific smoke tests out of :hyperdoc/tests and into :kioskbeerli/tests.
  5. Keep :dreyeck/kioskbeerli only as a downstream compatibility alias outside the HyperDoc core load path.
  6. Validate HyperDoc with :hyperdoc and :hyperdoc/executable-dita-tasks without registering or loading :kioskbeerli.
  7. Validate Kioskbeerli separately from the external target after its ASDF source registry is explicit.
