Konrad Feedback on Communication Pages

Feedback summary from Konrad (matrix: @khinsen:matrix.org):

In traditional human documentation, a narrowly scoped page like "what goes into hyperdoc/server" would usually be replaced by broader developer documentation that explains the overall architecture and subsystem responsibilities together.

Our current interpretation and decision:

  • Keep focused pages when they are useful for active collaboration and feedback exchange.
  • Extract stable parts into broader architecture/reference pages over time.
  • Treat robot assistants as part of the interaction system around this communication.
