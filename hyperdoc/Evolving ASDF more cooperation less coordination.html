Reading notes

The paper is useful here because it treats ASDF as more than a build tool. It describes a Lisp-specific boundary where loading, compiling, source discovery, output placement, and running-image state all interact. The HyperDoc lesson is that coordination pressure should become an inspectable protocol surface instead of an invisible social obligation.
