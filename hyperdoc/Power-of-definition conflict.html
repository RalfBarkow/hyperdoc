Power-of-definition conflict

hyperdoc

Power-of-definition conflict is the struggle over who gets to define the problem, the relevant values, and the legitimate interpretation of a social situation. HyperDoc uses this page for the difference between merely stating a position and gaining interpretive hegemony around it.

Why think tanks matter here

In the Denkpanzer material, think tanks matter less as neutral research shops than as organizations trying to shift what counts as common sense. Their success or failure is visible in the struggle over market radicality, legitimacy, and hegemony.

Conflict is visible in the communication around it

When actors fight over definitions, they produce bursts of commentary, rebuttal, reframing, and symbolic alignment. That visible thickening of communication is part of why definitional conflict can be read as an early warning sign.

Boundary

This page does not reduce all social conflict to discourse alone. The narrower point is that disputes over naming, framing, and legitimacy often reveal where deeper institutional or value conflicts are already active.
