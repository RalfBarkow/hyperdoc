Swiss think-tank model transfer

hyperdoc

This page names the frame in which an Anglo-Saxon think-tank pattern is read as entering Swiss institutional and media conditions. HyperDoc uses it for the difference between a model being imported and that model being accepted without friction.

What is being transferred

The transfer is not only a formal organization chart. It includes styles of policy intervention, media positioning, expert performance, and ideological coding that become legible as a recognizable think-tank pattern.

Why local reaction matters

A transfer model becomes sociologically interesting when local actors do not simply copy it. Support, rejection, irritation, and translation all reveal how the imported model is being reinterpreted inside another conflict field.

Boundary

This page does not claim a one-way import from one national setting into another. It names a heuristic for observing how organizational forms and their surrounding codes travel, mutate, and provoke response.
