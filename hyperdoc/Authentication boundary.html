Authentication boundary

hyperdoc

An Authentication boundary is a boundary where read-only or unauthenticated access gives way to explicit-auth guarded mutation or session-backed access. In HyperDoc it is important that this boundary stays explicit rather than being hidden inside a larger mutation surface.

Worked DMX example

The repair console is the current worked example. It keeps diagnosis on the left side and guarded mutation on the right side. The crossing condition is explicit: the operator must provide valid credentials for the current action.

  • username/password
  • authorization header
  • bearer token

This boundary should not be blurred with long-lived service config or with a general DMX login system. The repair console keeps credentials ephemeral and reuses the same guarded executor after the crossing.
