Think-tank ideological temporization

hyperdoc

This page preserves a Denkpanzer distinction: ideology is not only a fixed left/right label but also something staged through timing, attention, and recurring media situations. HyperDoc uses it for the temporal presentation of ideological differences in think-tank coverage.

What temporization means here

The paper does not ask only which ideology a think tank claims. It asks when and under what communication conditions certain ideological distinctions become visible, amplified, or strategically useful.

Search terms and attention patterns

One practical move in the paper is to use names of think tanks as search terms in the Swiss media arena, then compare which organizations attract attention and how ideological differences are timed and narrated around them.

Boundary

This page does not collapse ideology into media tactics alone. The narrower point is that timing, recurrence, and agenda-setting shape how ideological differences become publicly legible.
