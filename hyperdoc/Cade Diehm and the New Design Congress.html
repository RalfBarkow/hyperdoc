Who is being cited

Infosperber attributes the core warning to Cade Diehm, described there as a critical digital-thinker and technology specialist. New Design Congress describes itself as an independent research group confronting the gap between what digital societies say is happening and what is actually happening.

On its own site, New Design Congress presents Diehm as the organisation's founder and a researcher working across information security, interface politics, digital anthropology, identity, conflict, and infrastructure. That positioning matters because the resulting claims are not being made as neutral administrative guidance from the Swiss state; they come from a critical research and security-oriented perspective.

Why provenance matters here

HyperDoc uses this page to keep attribution explicit. When this cluster says “the article reports”, “Diehm argues”, or “the study claims”, this is the provenance node that explains whose claims those are and why they should be treated as argued positions rather than as automatic HyperDoc fact.

  • Self-(De)termination and Identifying & Defining the Digital Self: earlier notes that feed the article's framing of social engineering, ambiguous digital selfhood, and digital representation.
