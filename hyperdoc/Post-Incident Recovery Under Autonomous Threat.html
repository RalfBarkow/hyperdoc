Post-Incident Recovery Under Autonomous Threat

Recovery planning under autonomous-threat conditions means restoring safety, services, and trust while preserving evidence and accountability. The objective is resilient civic function, not retaliation.

Inspectable objects

  • Post-incident recovery under autonomous threat
  • Incident ledger and evidence
  • Service restoration prioritization
  • Community psychological recovery
  • After-action learning loop

Recovery phases

  1. Stabilize: secure people, sites, and immediate care.
  2. Preserve: capture logs, artifacts, and chain-of-custody evidence.
  3. Restore: bring up prioritized services with fallback operations.
  4. Review: publish post-incident findings and corrective actions.
  5. Adapt: update risk models, procedures, and training cycles.
