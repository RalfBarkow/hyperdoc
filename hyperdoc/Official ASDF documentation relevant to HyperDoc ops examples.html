Official ASDF documentation relevant to HyperDoc ops examples

There is no official ASDF concept named ops examples. In HyperDoc, that is a repo-local name for the ASDF subsystem :hyperdoc/examples/ops, described as "Ops and local example content for HyperDoc" and as a narrower layer for local or operational workflows.

Why these are the right links

HyperDoc's :hyperdoc/examples/ops is just an ordinary ASDF system boundary. So the relevant official ASDF concepts are: how a subsystem is defined with defsystem, how files and modules are declared as components, how one system depends on another, and what ASDF means by an operation such as load-op or test-op.

HyperDoc-specific conclusion

If you want to understand ops examples in your repository, read them as "examples that live in their own ASDF subsystem for operational/local material," not as an official ASDF feature category.
