Local source evidence

These compatibility claims now sit on top of a locally inspectable source path. The book-level evidence surface is (mind-and-mechanism-zotero-resolution-report), with the operational walkthrough on Resolve a local PDF from Zotero in HyperDoc. The report records whether this machine can currently resolve the title to a local attachment. The compatibility list below is editorial synthesis built on that source availability plus claims supported in the book itself.

Source-backed claims used here

  • The book defends a version of computationalism and treats computation as the main explanatory route into mind.
  • Its symbols-and-semantics discussion ties intentionality to thicker causal and informational relations than observer-imposed labels alone.
  • Its examples make representation matter when symbol structures and data structures alter what the system can do or predict.
  • Its self-model discussion treats introspection as the work of a model a system uses of itself, not as a magical extra channel.

Editorial compatibility seams

  1. Computation and computational explanation: the book's computationalist stance fits the existing Drew import because the repository already preserves software and planning artifacts, not just publication titles.
  2. Symbols, semantics, and intentionality: the book's chapter on symbols and semantics is directly relevant to the planning-language and ontology side of the Drew cluster, where symbol structures matter only if they can be given operational meaning.
  3. Representation that matters because it changes behavior: the book repeatedly treats representations as important when they guide or constrain action. That is the right bridge to PDDL, Opt, and Optop, where representational choices become planner behavior and not merely notation.
  4. Self-modeling as a modest bridge: the book's self-model discussion is useful to HyperDoc because it restates some introspection language in terms of models a system uses of itself. That is enough to make a narrow methodological connection to inspectable systems, including the smaller running-image coherence thread.

Boundary

Keep this slice narrow. Mind and Mechanism stays a page-level bridge/source. The reusable topic additions are limited to the exact-title concepts Computationalism, Symbols and semantics, Informational meaning, and Intentionality, if they are not already present under those canonical titles. Topics such as qualia, dualism, the Chinese Room, Mary the neuroscientist, or free will belong here only as page text unless a separate local neighborhood later makes one of them unavoidable.
