Critical reading to rewriting

hyperdoc

Kernighan/Plauger's base method is a concrete reader operation: study a real program example, name what makes it hard to read, rewrite it, compare before and after, and extract a style rule.

Base cycle

  1. Critical reading.
  2. Identify shortcomings.
  3. Rewrite.
  4. Compare before and after.
  5. Derive a rule.
  6. Record a reusable style lesson.

Identity matrix example

The built-in example is based on the Kernighan/Plauger identity-matrix case. It represents the original idea as a clever integer-division expression, the reader problem as reconstruction of intent, the rewrite as an explicit identity-matrix predicate, and the rule as writing clearly rather than being too clever.
