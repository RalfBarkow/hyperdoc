Failure surface

hyperdoc

A Failure surface is a first-class blocked or lossy surface that preserves failure evidence, classification, and adjacent next-step surfaces rather than burying them in prose or transient logs.

DMX worked example

The current worked example is the observed blocked workspace-journal case around carrier 927558 and companion 927568. The failure surface keeps the last reached stage prepare-transition, the failing mutation PUT /core/topic/927568, the 401 auth evidence, and the adjacent diagnostic/mutation surfaces together without pretending that the failure surface itself performs repair.

Why failure surface matters

  • It makes blocked state inspectable as data.
  • It keeps adjacent next-step surfaces explicit.
  • It preserves the current guarded behavior instead of widening permissions in order to hide the failure.
