hyperdoc

Ben Lynn's browser runner and HyperDoc's Common Lisp reconstruction answer related reading questions through different evaluators. This page keeps that distinction executable and visible.

Why the upstream page is materialized locally

The upstream response at Ben Lynn's Lisp page sends X-Frame-Options: DENY and Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none'. A remote iframe is therefore neither a reliable nor an intended integration. HyperDoc instead serves six SHA-256-pinned files from a Nix fixed-output asset at the stable local route /roots-of-lisp-lynn/lambda/lisp.html.
